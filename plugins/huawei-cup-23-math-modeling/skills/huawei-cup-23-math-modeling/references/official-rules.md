# Official rule digest for Huawei Cup 23

This is a dated working digest. Re-check the current official page and attachments before every live submission.

## Contest and timeline observed in the supplied official announcement

- Contest: “华为杯”第二十三届中国研究生数学建模竞赛, 2026.
- Questions: choose one from A, B, C, D, E, F.
- Competition: 2026-09-23 08:00 through 2026-09-27 12:00.
- MD5 submission window: 2026-09-26 12:00 through 2026-09-27 12:00.
- PDF upload window: 2026-09-27 14:00 through 2026-09-28 24:00.
- Attachment upload window: 2026-09-29 08:00 through 2026-09-30 24:00.
- The announcement says the PDF must be uploaded uncompressed, then the system’s Submit action must be completed.
- Paper filename: selected question letter plus team number, e.g. `A26000010001.pdf`; attachment archive follows the same stem and is limited to 50 MB.
- The MD5 identifies the exact PDF bytes. After submitting MD5, do not modify that PDF; any modification requires a fresh MD5 and audit.

## Format specification and template

- Use the official template. Preserve the cover page, including all four logos; do not replace or remove them.
- The first page is the cover. The second page starts the unified abstract page and body.
- Abstract should include modeling idea, main methods, models, results/conclusions, innovation, and keywords; normally no more than two pages and no English translation is required.
- Page numbering starts at 1 on the abstract page, runs continuously, and is centered in the footer.
- No header. After the cover, no school, names, team number, or other identity marker may appear.
- Title: three-point boldface Chinese title; level-one headings: four-point boldface and centered. Other Chinese text: small-four Song typeface; single line spacing. Verify the exact rendered template because local Word font substitution can change appearance.
- Citations use bracketed reference numbers such as `[1]` and `[3]`; books require page numbers. References are ordered by first appearance. The official specification gives distinct formats for books, journal papers, and web resources, including access date.

## AI tool and output regulation

- AI may assist information gathering, ideation, modeling analysis, data processing, writing, and programming, but may not replace independent thinking or core innovation.
- Understand and rewrite AI output in the team’s own language.
- Models and formulas must have a derivation and a formal/reliable source. Unverifiable AI-generated formulas/models are risky and may be rejected; never present them as established facts.
- For AI-assisted data analysis, annotate the relevant result with tool name, version/model, developer/company, and release date.
- For AI-assisted code, add the required comment before the program with the same tool metadata.
- If the question requires it, provide AI inputs and post-processing strategy, including frameworks, open-source software, technical route, assumptions, parameters, and hyperparameters.
- Violations can cancel award eligibility.

## Discipline and privacy

- During the contest, do not discuss the problem with teachers or people outside the team, copy or buy papers, or publish the paper for online similarity checking.
- The final package must be anonymous except for the required cover page. Treat team identifiers and member names as sensitive project data.
